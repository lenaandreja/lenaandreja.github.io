A few words about me

I'm Olena (Lena) Andreichuk, a wedding photographer based in Vienna. For more than 13 years I have been photographing couples, and I never look for the staged pose. I look for the small, true moments: a quiet glance, a familiar smile, a tender gesture.

I notice the details that get lost in the rush of a wedding day, so you can look back and feel how it really was. Vienna is home, but I'm happy to travel to wherever you celebrate.
